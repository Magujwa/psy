About the practice

Psychological care grounded in understanding.

N.Z Psychological Services provides professional psychological support for individuals and families navigating personal, emotional and relational challenges.

Sessions are designed to provide a calm, respectful and confidential environment where you can explore what you are experiencing and work toward meaningful change.

Registered by the Eswatini Medical and Dental Council

More information about my qualifications, professional registration and areas of practice will be provided upon request.

Our approach

Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

Are sessions available online?

Yes. N.Z Psychological Services offers both in-person and online sessions.

Where are in-person sessions held?

In-person sessions are held at Mbabane Club.

How do I make a booking?

All appointments are arranged through WhatsApp. Contact the practice to enquire about available appointments.

Is Oracle Medical Aid accepted?

Oracle Medical Aid is accepted, subject to the applicable medical aid benefits, requirements and authorisation.

Can I pay cash?

Yes. Cash payment is accepted.

What happens if I need to cancel?

For cash appointments, cancellations must be communicated at least two days before the scheduled service. Where the required notice is not provided, the booking deposit is non-refundable.
